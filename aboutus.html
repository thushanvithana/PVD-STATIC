Our Story

Founded in 2010, PVD Grocery Store has grown from a small local market to one of the leading grocery chains in the region.

Our journey began with a simple mission: to provide fresh, high-quality groceries at affordable prices while delivering exceptional customer service. Today, we continue that tradition with multiple locations and an extensive online shopping experience.

We take pride in sourcing our products from local farmers and trusted suppliers who share our commitment to quality and sustainability.
